# App Ignore Rules

Source: [packages/examples/app/.gitignore](../../../packages/examples/app/.gitignore).

## What

Exclusions for frontend dependencies, output, logs and editor-local files.

## Why

Generated/local artifacts are not template source.

## How

Ignores node_modules, dist, local files and most editor state while preserving .vscode/extensions.json. These rules supplement repository-level secret exclusions.
